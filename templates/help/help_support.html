Frequently Asked Questions

What does my 68% High Risk Readmission score mean? expand_more

The 68% score is a statistical risk estimate calculated from your clinical history (such as prior inpatient admissions, elevated serum creatinine, and current medication count). It helps your care team schedule priority 72-hour primary care follow-ups.

Is my confidential medical history exposed on the public QR code? expand_more

No. The QR code contains only a randomized cryptographic verification identifier. When scanned, it confirms only credential authenticity, issuing hospital, and validity dates. Your detailed clinical records and diagnoses are never publicly accessible.

How do I switch the application language to हिन्दी (Hindi)? expand_more

Click the language toggle button located in the top navigation bar at any time. The entire user interface, clinical summaries, and video consultation subtitles will instantly switch between English and Hindi.
