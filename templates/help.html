Clinical Decision Support Guide

Frequently asked questions, clinical protocol integration, and system documentation.

help How does HRP Clinical calculate the 30-day readmission risk?

The model utilizes a gradient-boosted decision tree ensemble (XGBoost) trained on 30,000 multi-center electronic health records. It processes patient demographics, physiological vitals, acute biomarker labs (such as creatinine and hemoglobin), prior utilization frequency, and active medication counts to predict the mathematical probability of unplanned return within 30 days of discharge.

medical_services What are the recommended actions for High-Risk patients?

For patients stratified into the High Risk tier (>60%), hospital clinical protocol mandates:

  • Primary care follow-up appointment confirmed within 72 hours of discharge.
  • Pharmacist-led discharge medication reconciliation to mitigate polypharmacy errors.
  • Home health agency or visiting nurse transition-of-care order.
  • Patient provided with 24/7 clinical condition hotline number.
